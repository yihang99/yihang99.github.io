Research

My current research focuses on embodied intelligence, aiming to enable robots to perform generalizable manipulation tasks in the real world. I am especially interested in transferring human's learning paradigm to embodied human-like robots, to achieve efficient skill acquisition and generalization in various real-world scenarios.

Publications

OpenHLM: An Empirical Recipe for Whole-Body Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

Yingdong Hu*, Haodong Zhu*, Boyuan Zheng*, Yihang Hu*, Tong Zhang*, Zunhao Chen, Junming Zhao, Ruiqian Nai, Yang Gao

CoRL, 2026

Focus: Develops a whole-body humanoid loco-manipulation recipe that maps language and visual observations into coordinated full-body actions. The work emphasizes long-horizon real-world tasks where locomotion, manipulation, and perception must be handled together rather than as separate skills.

HuMI: Humanoid Whole-Body Manipulation from Humanoid-Free Demonstrations

Ruiqian Nai*, Boyuan Zheng*, Junming Zhao*, Haodong Zhu, Sicong Dai, Zunhao Chen, Yihang Hu, Yingdong Hu, Tong Zhang, Chuan Wen, Yang Gao

CoRL, 2026

Focus: Introduces a portable humanoid-free interface for collecting rich whole-body human demonstrations and translating them into feasible humanoid skills. The system reduces the dependence on physical robot teleoperation, making it easier to gather diverse demonstrations for whole-body manipulation.

Prior Reinforce: Goal-Conditioned Dynamic Manipulation with Limited Trials

Yihang Hu*, Pingyue Sheng*, Yuyang Liu, Shengjie Wang, Yang Gao

IROS, 2026

Focus: Proposes a goal-conditioned framework that learns a motion prior from limited demonstrations and refines behavior through feedback-driven optimization. The method targets dynamic manipulation with few real-world trials, making reinforcement learning more practical under scarce interaction data.

TimeRewarder: Learning Dense Reward from Passive Videos via Frame-wise Temporal Distance

Yuyang Liu*, Chuan Wen*, Yihang Hu, Dinesh Jayaraman, Yang Gao

ICML, 2026

Focus: Learns dense reward signals from passive videos by estimating frame-wise temporal progress toward task completion. By turning ordinary videos into reward supervision, the method reduces reliance on hand-designed rewards and supports more scalable robot learning.

Tactile-VLA: Unlocking Vision-Language-Action Model's Physical Knowledge for Tactile Generalization

Jialei Huang*, Shuo Wang*, Fanqi Lin, Yihang Hu, Chuan Wen, Yang Gao

arXiv preprint, 2025

Focus: Extends vision-language-action models with tactile sensing and hybrid position-force control for contact-rich manipulation. The work shows how physical feedback can improve generalization when visual observations alone are insufficient for fine-grained interaction.

SKIL: Semantic Keypoint Imitation Learning for Generalizable Data-efficient Manipulation

Shengjie Wang, Jiacheng You, Yihang Hu, Jiongye Li, Yang Gao

RSS, 2025

Focus: Uses vision foundation models to extract semantic keypoints as compact task representations for imitation learning. These object-centric representations help reduce sample complexity and improve transfer across objects, scenes, and manipulation setups.
